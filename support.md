# Geòlas — Support

Geòlas is a field notebook for geologists covering Great Britain: log a site with GPS, get bedrock and superficial geology from the British Geological Survey, and keep photos, samples and notes synced privately through your own iCloud.

## Getting help

Email callumpmaclellan@proton.me. Say which device you're on and what you were doing, and include a screenshot if there's anything to see.

## Common questions

**Where are my sites kept?**
On your device, in the app's own storage — and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account too, so your devices share one library. There's no Geòlas account and no Geòlas server. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Why is there no geology data for my site?**
Bedrock and superficial geology lookups currently only cover Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales). You can still log a site anywhere — you just won't get an automatic geology lookup outside that coverage.

**Some of my sites are grouped under the wrong region — why?**
Region grouping for your own sites is inferred from location bands, and it isn't perfect at a few boundaries. The curated reference sites always show their correct region regardless.

**How do I get my sites off the app?**
Settings has a JSON and GeoJSON export you can share out through the usual share sheet.

**Can I use it on my Mac as well as my phone?**
Yes — it runs on the Mac through Catalyst. It's the same app, not a separate one, and it's the same iCloud sync.

**Will my sites be on all my devices?**
Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account. It syncs through your own iCloud account, not through any server of mine — see the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## What Geòlas does not do

No accounts, no subscription, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
