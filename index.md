# Geòlas

A field notebook for geologists, covering Great Britain. Log a site with GPS, get bedrock and superficial geology straight from the British Geological Survey, and keep photos, samples and notes — synced privately between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
